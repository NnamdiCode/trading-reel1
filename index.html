MOBILE WORKFLOW

How to record

  1. Pick an account. Tap one of the five cards on the studio screen.
  2. Start the screen recorder first. Android: Quick Settings → Screen record. iPhone: Control Center → Screen Recording.
  3. Tap Start recording. The studio hides, a 3-second countdown plays, then the PNL starts climbing.
  4. Wait for the hold. The profit figure sits at your target for about 2 seconds. Stop the recording after that.
  5. Return and check it off. Hold the top-left corner for 1 second, then tap the account's checkmark.

The take loops automatically if you need another pass. Your 5 accounts and checkmarks are saved on this device for the day.

iPhone / iPad: add this page to your Home Screen first and launch it from the icon, or Safari's toolbar will appear in the recording. Keep Auto-Lock set to Never.
